# Safety notes

XOSINT V2 is a public-profile search helper. Use it only for lawful and ethical purposes.

- A matching name or username is not proof of identity.
- Do not use search results to harass, stalk, threaten, dox, impersonate, or target people.
- Do not attempt to access private accounts, bypass access controls, or collect credentials.
- Follow the terms of service and rate limits of each platform.
- Manually verify any result before drawing conclusions.
